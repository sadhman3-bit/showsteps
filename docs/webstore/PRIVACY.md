# Trailmark privacy policy

_Last updated: 12 October 2026_

Trailmark is a browser extension (and an optional Windows app) that records a process you perform and turns it into a step-by-step guide.

## What Trailmark handles

While **you** are recording, and only then, Trailmark reads the page you are recording:

- screenshots of the visible tab;
- the clicked or typed-into element (its name, label and position), and what you typed into ordinary fields;
- the page's address and title;
- the positions of words on screen, so you can find and blur sensitive details later.

Trailmark **never stores** what you type into password fields, one-time-code fields or payment card fields.

When you use **Guide Me** or **Run for me**, Trailmark reads the page you are on to find the element each step refers to.

## Where it goes

Everything stays on your computer. Guides, screenshots and settings are saved in your browser's own storage on this device, or in a folder you choose. If you turn on the connection to the Trailmark desktop app, the extension and the app talk to each other directly on this computer.

Trailmark has **no servers and no accounts**. It sends nothing to the developer or to anyone else. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking. Text recognition (OCR) and the optional AI writing tools run on your device.

A guide leaves your computer only when **you** export or share it (for example as a PDF, Word or HTML file), and then only to where you put it.

## Sale and use of data

Your data is not sold, not transferred to third parties, not used for advertising, and not used for any purpose unrelated to making and following guides. It is not used to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

## Your control

- Website access is requested when you first record, not at install, and you can withdraw it in Chrome at any time.
- You can delete any guide in the library, or everything at once in Trailmark's Settings ("Delete all Trailmark data").
- Removing the extension deletes the data it stored in your browser.
